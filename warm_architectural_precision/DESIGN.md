## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the disciplined rigor of architectural practice combined with the Mediterranean warmth of southern Spain. It is built for discerning property owners, international investors, and public and private developers seeking structural integrity, sustainability, and contemporary elegance.

The visual style merges **Warm Architectural Minimalism** with **Tectonic Structure**:
- **Tectonic Clarity:** Layouts mimic architectural blueprints and structural elevations, balancing precise grids, thin perimeter dividing lines, and generous negative space.
- **Mediterranean Warmth:** Off-white lime-plaster surfaces and sun-baked terracotta accents soften technical austerity into a tactile, inviting studio experience.
- **Material Authenticity:** Flat, crisp surfaces with authentic photographic treatments that showcase real textures—polished concrete, limestone, timber, and sun-cast shadows—avoiding unnecessary decorative artifacts or artificial gradients.

## Colors

The color palette reflects architectural masonry, charcoal structural steel, and Andalusian terracotta:

- **Primary (`#1A1A1A`):** Deep charcoal/anthracite. Used for primary typography, crisp structural hairpins, key architectural outlines, and solid inverted high-contrast conversion panels.
- **Secondary (`#C85A32`):** Sun-baked terracotta copper. Reserved for high-intent conversion CTAs, interactive highlights, key metric badges, and focal anchor points.
- **Tertiary (`#B85028`):** Deep burnished copper. Used for hover and pressed states on secondary accents, as well as focus rings and deep-contrast editorial emphasis.
- **Neutral (`#F3F3F0`):** Architectural stone grey. Applied as secondary surface container fills, table headers, card backgrounds, and technical framing.
- **Background Base (`#FBFBF9`):** Lime plaster off-white canvas. Provides a warm, glare-free foundation that enhances architectural renders and photography.
- **Structural Lines (`#E5E5E0`):** Muted hairline dividers and container outlines that evoke drafting grid lines without competing with content.

## Typography

The typographical pairing anchors geometric clarity with reading comfort:

- **Display & Headings (Outfit):** Characterized by balanced geometric apertures, low stroke contrast, and architectural proportions. Used to convey modern spatial concepts and bold portfolio titles.
- **Body & Captions (Manrope):** A structured, modern grotesque with exceptional legibility across dense project specs, floor plan annotations, and bilingual text runs.
- **Technical Annotations:** Project numbers, geographic coordinates (e.g., Málaga latitude/longitude), and floor space metrics use uppercase `label-md` or `technical-mono` with expanded letter spacing to evoke architectural documentation stamps.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a modular 12-column architectural grid defined by generous horizontal spans and breathing room:

- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12-column grid with a maximum content canvas width of 1440px, `2rem` (32px) gutters, and `4rem` (64px) lateral margins. Editorial sections can span asymmetrically (e.g., 5-column editorial text alongside a 7-column architectural elevation).
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column grid with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters and `2rem` (32px) lateral margins. Split modules reflow into balanced dual-column or full-width blocks.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid grid with `1rem` (16px) gutters and `1.25rem` (20px) outer edge margins. Touch elements stretch across full column modules or clean stacked blocks.
- **Vertical Cadence:** Large transitions between narrative sections employ `space-3xl` (96px), ensuring spatial separation akin to room transitions in modern architecture.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy, artificial drop shadows in favor of **structural line bounds and daylight ambient diffusion**:

- **Hairline Framing:** Surface levels are divided primarily by delicate `1px solid #E5E5E0` outlines. This creates crisp, plan-like definition while retaining a clean, flat aesthetic.
- **Surface Layering:** Depth is conveyed by contrasting `#FBFBF9` (base canvas) with `#F3F3F0` (recessed containers, drawing bays, and spec sheets) and `#1A1A1A` (dramatic full-bleed dark sections).
- **Daylight Ambient Elevation:** For floating interaction states (such as sticky inquiry docks, modal overlays, or project inspection trays), elevation uses soft, diffused ambient light that simulates Mediterranean natural sun:
  - *Resting Card:* Flat border `1px solid #E5E5E0`, no shadow.
  - *Hovered Card / Active Inspector:* `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.07)`, subtle `-2px` translateY transform.
  - *Modal & Overlay:* `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.14)`.

## Shapes

The shape hierarchy employs restrained corner radii that balance the precision of structural drafting with contemporary ergonomic softness:

- **Base Radius (`0.25rem` / 4px):** Applied to compact technical tags, inputs, and architectural badges.
- **Medium Radius (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Used on primary buttons, interactive controls, and form containers (`rounded-lg`).
- **Container Radius (`0.75rem` / 12px):** Applied to featured cards, architectural project framing, modal dialogue sheets, and media containers (`rounded-xl`).
- **Architectural Framing:** Image frames never use circular or exaggerated pill cutouts; they maintain rectangular discipline with `0.5rem` to `0.75rem` perimeter rounding to preserve building silhouettes and horizon lines.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (High-Conversion):** Background `#C85A32`, text `#FFFFFF`, border none, minimum height `48px` (exceeding touch accessibility requirements), horizontal padding `24px` (`space-lg`), shape `rounded-lg` (8px). Hover: background `#B85028`. Active focus: 3px outer ring in `#C85A32` at 30% opacity.
- **Secondary Action (Technical/Editorial):** Background `#1A1A1A`, text `#FBFBF9`, min-height `48px`. Hover: background `#2E2E2E`.
- **Ghost/Outline Action:** Background transparent, border `1px solid #1A1A1A`, text `#1A1A1A`. Hover: background `#F3F3F0`.

### Cards (Project & Service Showcases)
- **Structure:** Background `#F3F3F0` or `#FBFBF9`, border `1px solid #E5E5E0`, corner radius `rounded-xl` (12px), inner padding `space-lg` (24px).
- **Image Integration:** Full-width or top-anchored architectural imagery with a 16:10 or 4:3 aspect ratio, subtle zoom (`scale: 1.02`) on card hover, and clean typography placed below or within defined structural grid cells.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Input Fields:** Minimum height `48px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E5E5E0`, padding `0 16px`, corner radius `rounded-lg` (8px), text `#1A1A1A` (`body-md`), placeholder `#8C8C88`.
- **Active State:** Border `#1A1A1A` with a subtle outline glow `0 0 0 1px #1A1A1A`.
- **Labels:** Positioned outside top, `label-lg`, font weight 600, color `#1A1A1A`.

### Badges, Tags & Technical Chips
- **Style:** Compact pill-box or soft-corner (`rounded`) structure with uppercase tracking.
- **Palette:** Background `#F3F3F0`, text `#1A1A1A`, border `1px solid #E5E5E0`, height `28px`, padding `0 10px`, typography `label-md`.
- **Status/Highlight Chip:** Background `rgba(200, 90, 50, 0.1)`, text `#C85A32`, border `1px solid rgba(200, 90, 50, 0.2)`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Touch Target:** Minimum `44x44px` invisible container with a centered `20x20px` visual control.
- **Appearance:** Border `1.5px solid #1A1A1A`, background `#FFFFFF`, radius `4px` (checkbox) or circular (radio).
- **Checked State:** Background `#1A1A1A` (or `#C85A32` for key legal confirmations) with white iconography.

### Specialized Architectural Components
- **Project Blueprint Drawer:** A collapsible technical drawer detailing project specifications (m², completion year, energy rating, location) styled with lightweight hairline divider rules (`#E5E5E0`) and tabular numeric typography.
- **High-Contrast Dark Canvas Section:** Inverted section with background `#1A1A1A`, text `#FBFBF9`, and border `#2E2E2E`, housing closing high-intent consultation CTAs with secondary terracotta button accents.
